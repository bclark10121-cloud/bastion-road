## Made for this game

- The painted map, towers, robots and guard drone: pictures made with Google Gemini for this game, then cut out and resized (`art_src/`).
- Sound effects: made by `tools/make_sfx.py` with a small synth.
- Menu sounds: made by the studio starter's sound tool.
