Bastion Road

Hold the outpost against the robot waves

Robot waves land at the edges of a jungle outpost and march to the gate. Build four kinds of toy towers on the pads, send guard drones to hold robots on the road and pick the right tower for armored, shielded and flying robots. Send waves early for bonus credits and win up to three stars.

What kind of game is this?

Kingdom Rush style tower defense. Fixed pads, a ring of towers around each one and robots on the march.
Guard drones. The Drone Bay sends three drones to hold robots on the road. Move their flag to set the trap.
Right tower, right job. Blasters for fast and flying robots, Arc Coils for armor, Mortars for groups. Shields shrug off energy.
Painted jungle world. Toy render towers and robots on a hand-painted map, with sounds and music.

Things to do

  • Hold Landing Zone through ten waves from two landings.
  • Build Blasters, Drone Bays, Arc Coils and Mortars, and upgrade each to level 3.
  • Move the Drone Bay flag to hold robots where your towers hit hardest.
  • Learn each new robot from its card the first time it lands.
  • Send waves early for bonus credits.
  • Speed the game up to 2x.
  • Win up to three stars and beat your best score.
  • Pick Easy, Normal or Hard in Settings.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse
Click a pad, then click a choice twice to build. 1 to 4 pick ring choices. Arrows move the cursor and Enter picks. Space sends the next wave. F changes speed. Backspace goes back. Esc opens the Menu.
Xbox controller
D-pad or left stick picks a pad or a landing beacon. A opens the ring, then A twice on a choice. B goes back. Y sends the next wave. X changes speed. To move a flag, the left stick aims and A places. View or Menu opens the Menu.
Touch
Tap a pad, then tap a choice twice. Tap a red beacon to send the next wave. To move a flag, tap the road inside the circle. Play with your phone held sideways.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Bastion Road. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on this page.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser if your phone asks.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.3.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
