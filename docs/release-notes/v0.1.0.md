# Bastion Road v0.1.0: gray box

Released 2026-11-20. Kit 1.22.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## What is new

- The first playable build of Bastion Road, a tower defense on a sci-fi outpost.
- One level, Landing Zone: eight build pads and a road that splits in two and joins again before the gate.
- Two towers: the Blaster (fast shots at one robot) and the Mortar (slow shells that hit a group). Sell a tower for 60 percent back.
- Two robots, Crawlers and Scuttlers, over six waves. Each wave is tougher than the last.
- Lives and defeat. Lose all lives and the level ends. Retry starts it again. Your stars and best score always stay.
- Win with up to three stars. Send a wave early for bonus credits. Play at 1x or 2x speed.
- Easy, Normal and Hard in Settings.
- Held upright, the map turns so the road runs up the screen.
- Full Xbox controller play: the D-pad picks a pad, A builds, Y sends a wave.
- Gray box shapes and stand-in sounds. Toy render art comes in 0.3.
