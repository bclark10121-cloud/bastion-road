# Bastion Road v0.2.0: the fight

Released 2026-11-20. Kit 1.22.0. Godot 4.7.2. First release of plan v2 (Kingdom Rush style).

## What is new

- Four towers, each with three levels: Blaster (fast shots, hits flyers), Drone Bay (three guard drones hold robots on the road), Arc Coil (energy bolts that ignore armor and jump to a second robot) and Mortar (shells that hit a group, ground only).
- Guard drones fight robots on the road and hold them in place. Move a Drone Bay's flag to set where they stand. Downed drones come back after 10 seconds.
- Armor and shields. Armor cuts Blaster and drone damage. Shields cut Arc Coil damage. Mortars count half the armor.
- Five robots: Crawler, Scuttler, Plated Walker (armored), Warden (shielded) and Hover Drone (flies over guards and Mortars). Each new robot gets a short card the first time it lands.
- Landing Zone is redrawn with two landings that join before the gate, 12 pads and 10 waves.
- A red beacon at each landing shows the next wave's robots. Tap it to send the wave early for bonus credits.
- A ring of choices around each pad, Kingdom Rush style: tap once to see it, tap again to buy. Upgrade and sell from the same ring.
- First effects: towers pop up when built, robots flash when hit, credits float up from each kill, shells arc and burst, arcs crackle.
- Landscape only. Held upright, the game asks you to turn your phone.
- New battle engine: a fixed 60 steps a second, so 2x speed plays exactly like 1x, just faster.
