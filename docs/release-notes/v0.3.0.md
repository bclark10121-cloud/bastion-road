# Bastion Road v0.3.0: the look

Released 2026-11-20. Kit 1.22.0. Godot 4.7.2. Built on your 0.3 picks: jungle planet, Audiowide, Heroic Age and Movement Proposition.

## What is new

- Landing Zone is a painted jungle map in the clean toy render style: teal grass, mushroom trees, a waterfall and the outpost wall and gate.
- The map has a new shape, traced from the painting: a west landing with a road that splits around a grassy island and a north landing that joins below it. Robots from the west take either side of the island in turn.
- Toy render towers: Blaster, Drone Bay, Arc Coil and Mortar, each with its own look at level 1, 2 and 3. Towers pop up when built and grow when upgraded.
- Toy render robots: Crawler, Scuttler, Plated Walker, Warden in its shield bubble and Hover Drone, plus the friendly guard drones. Robots bob as they walk, face the way they go and flash when hit. Robots pass behind taller towers.
- The landing beacons show the next wave's robots as small pictures.
- Sounds: tower build, upgrade, sell, Blaster shots, Arc Coil zaps, Mortar launches and blasts, robot scrap, credit chimes, the wave siren and a warning when a robot reaches the gate.
- Music: Heroic Age on the start screen and Movement Proposition in battle.
- A new start screen: the painted map behind the Audiowide title, a Blaster firing at a walking Crawler and a Hover Drone crossing the sky.
- A new app icon: the level 3 Blaster on the jungle map.
- The map is bigger on screen. The HUD sits over the map's edges.
- The version on the start screen now reads correctly (0.1 and 0.2 showed 0.1.0).

## Judgment calls

- Gemini would not take a layout picture in the browser panel, so the painting came first and the roads and pads were traced from it. The map's shape changed from 0.2.
- Towers and robots are Gemini pictures cut out as sprites, not Meshy models baked to sprites. Meshy baking can come later for turning turrets and walk cycles.
- Robots bob and flip instead of having walk frames.
- The outpost is a wall and gate on the right, not the full base from the concept.
- The icon was made from the new art without a decision page. Ask for options if you want to pick.

## Known limits

- Not tested on a real phone, real touch or a real controller.
- Normal: the bot wins with 10 of 20 lives. Hard: the bot wins with 2 lives.
