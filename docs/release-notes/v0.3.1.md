# Bastion Road v0.3.1: fixes and balance

Released 2026-11-21. Kit 1.22.0. Godot 4.7.2. Release A of plan v3 (`ideas/n14/PLAN_v3.md` in game-studio), from the six-agent review of 0.3.0.

## What is new

### Fixes
- The red tint after a robot reaches the gate now fades. Before, it stayed on the map for the rest of the level and after Retry.
- Music loops. Both tracks stopped after about two minutes. The silence at the end of each track is trimmed so the loop is tight.
- The Arc Coil at level 3 now jumps to two new robots. Before, the jump could bounce back to the first robot and stop.
- The build pop grows past full size and settles. Before, it jumped in one frame.
- Lightning and muzzle flashes no longer freeze under the win or defeat panel.
- A robot held by guard drones keeps facing the way it walked.
- Blaster bolts and Mortar shells move smoothly on 120 Hz screens.
- A new wave banner no longer cuts the last one short.
- Tester tools: Go to wave now frees the guard drones too.
- The early-call pop shows at the landing the wave comes from.

### The map
- Five pads moved so every pad reaches the road: pads 2, 6, 7, 10 and 11. Pad 10 now guards the gate. Pad 11 sits inside the bend by the gate road. Before, pads 10 and 11 could not reach the road and a Drone Bay there could not move its flag.

### Balance
- Arc Coil levels 2 and 3 hit about 25 percent softer.
- Wardens block 80 percent of energy damage (was 60). Plated Walkers block 70 percent of physical damage (was 60).
- The Mortar fires every 2.4 s (was 3.0) and hits about 60 percent harder with a wider blast.
- Guard drones hit harder at every level.
- Robot health: Normal 110 percent (was 100), Hard 130 percent (was 108). Easy stays at 80.
- Wave 2 opens the north road with four slow Crawlers. The fast Scuttlers wait for wave 3.
- Wave 8 is a tight swarm of 16 Crawlers plus 4 Wardens. Mortars and guard drones shine here.
- Early calls pay 3 credits per second skipped (was 1.5).
- New: every wave after the first brings a 20 credit supply drop.

What the test bots show now:
- No single tower type wins Hard. Each one alone loses.
- A mixed build wins Easy, Normal and Hard.
- A new player who builds one of each tower left to right wins Normal.
- A level now has 18 to 22 purchases, up from about 12.

### Sound
- A brass outpost horn plays for each wave. It replaces the loud siren.
- A bigger horn and "Final wave!" banner play on wave 10.
- A win sting and a defeat sting. The battle music fades out under them and comes back on Retry.
- The Arc Coil zap, the guard drone clang and the gate warning are louder and clearer on phone speakers.
- Credit chimes play at most four times a second, so fast kills no longer ring nonstop.

## Judgment calls

- The supply drop is new. Kingdom Rush has no exact match. It fixes long waits with nothing to buy. Set `wave_pay` to 0 in `data/levels/landing_zone.json` to turn it off.
- The block test now reads armor and shield values from the data, so balance changes never break it. It still checks the math.
- Pads 6 and 7 also moved a little, since they barely reached the road.
- Balance numbers come from bot runs. A person plays differently. Your notes decide the next pass.
